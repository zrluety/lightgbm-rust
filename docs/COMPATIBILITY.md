# Compatibility matrix

Target: LightGBM **v4.7.0** (commit `8f7036f`, see [UPSTREAM.md](UPSTREAM.md)). State as of release 0.3.0 (Tier 1 added: `r2`/`average_precision`/`auc_mu`, `Booster.refit`, monotone and interaction constraints, DART, random forest, and CSV/TSV/LibSVM text files). The numbers come from `tests/report/summary.md` (regenerate with the commands in [TESTING.md](TESTING.md)).

**lightgbm-rust does not have full parity with LightGBM.** It implements a CPU subset with numerical and categorical features: GBDT, DART and random forest; the regression objectives, binary, multiclass and multiclass-OVA, and ranking (`lambdarank`, `rank_xendcg`); bagging, GOSS, feature subsampling, extra trees, and monotone and interaction constraints; data from arrays, pandas, pyarrow, polars, scipy.sparse, binary Dataset files and CSV/TSV/LibSVM text files. Within that subset, results match upstream bit for bit on every single-threaded differential case run so far. Everything outside the subset either raises `LightGBMError("not supported by lightgbm-rust yet: ...")` or is absent.

## How to read this

Each feature is assessed on four separate axes:

| axis | meaning |
|---|---|
| **API** | the Python names, signatures, defaults, and error types/messages match `lightgbm` 4.7.0 |
| **Behavior** | the numerical results match upstream (bins, gradients, trees, predictions, metrics) |
| **Model format** | the model text written and read is interchangeable with upstream |
| **Performance** | speed and memory are comparable with upstream |

Each axis takes one of these statuses:
- `verified`: implemented, with differential or upstream-test evidence cited in the row.
- `implemented`: implemented and covered by our own tests, without upstream-comparison evidence.
- `partial`: some of the feature works; the gaps are listed.
- `not started`
- `n/a`

A dash in the Tolerance column means the comparison is exact or there is no numerical output. Named tolerances refer to sections of [`tests/tolerances.toml`](../tests/tolerances.toml).

The **evidence** referred to throughout comes from three places:
- **Differential tests (D):** `tests/differential`, run against the PyPI `lightgbm==4.7.0` wheel. These are 193 cases of 3,000 to 8,000 rows with 6 to 12 features, run single-threaded plus 4-thread runs for 18 of them, and 9 of them also continued through `init_model` in 3 ways and 7 of them also trained from pyarrow and polars input. 9 cases (8 of them plus a variant whose most frequent bin is not the zero bin) are also trained, validated and predicted from 5 scipy.sparse layouts. 15 of the cases use categorical features, and 3 of those are also trained from pandas category columns with 3 ways of naming the categorical features. Those layouts are CSR, CSC, float32 CSR with int64 `indptr`, CSR with explicit zeros, and COO. Every case also compares SHAP values (`pred_contrib`), and 4 of the sparse cases compare sparse SHAP output in 3 layouts. On top of these come 14 ranking cases (`lambdarank`, `rank_xendcg`; 150 queries of 1 to 39 rows, about 3,000 rows), of which 3 also run through `cv` and `subset` and 4 run at 4 threads. The scikit-learn estimators add 17 estimator cases (regression, binary and multiclass classification with `predict_proba`, `LGBMModel`, custom objectives and metrics, class weights, pandas categoricals, a learning-rate schedule), 3 `LGBMRanker` variants, and continued training, cloning and pickling. 4 cases also reset the learning rate during training, and 48 more reset other parameters, the objective or the training set: 33 per-round parameter schedules (4 of them also through `cv`), 5 objective or `seed` switches and 10 training-set replacements. 15 metric cases cover `r2`, `average_precision` and `auc_mu`, also at 4 threads, and 13 cases are refit at 3 decay rates (plus one case covering 3 refit variants). The DART and random-forest cases also run through `update`, `rollback_one_iter` and late-added validation sets. 14 text-file cases load CSV, TSV and LibSVM files (headers, label/weight/group/ignore columns, side files, `two_round`), and further text-file tests cover continued training, binary files, prediction from files and 27 error messages. 19 engine-log scenarios compare every line a registered logger receives at `verbosity=2`, and 26 plotting cases compare split-value histograms, tree digraphs, the drawn matplotlib axes and rendered trees. 109 feature-group storage cases cover bundled, sparse and multi-value bins: 3 sparse datasets x 6 storage variants x row-wise / col-wise at 1 and 4 threads (72), the same datasets loaded from CSR, CSC, CSV (with and without `two_round`) and LibSVM (30), plus dump, binary-file and forced-split cases. 222 quantized-gradient cases train 4 objectives under 13 variants, row-wise and col-wise at 1 and 4 threads, plus sparse groups, weighted regression and lambdarank. There are 111,776 comparisons over 853 cases. All are bitwise exact except 153 four-thread values of the pointwise metrics (`l2`, `r2`, `poisson`, `huber`, `binary_logloss`, `multi_logloss`). Of these, 2 come from parameter-reset schedules, 36 from the validation curves of the storage cases and 108 from those of the quantized cases; the models and predictions of all of these cases are exact. In each, upstream's OpenMP reduction adds the per-thread sums in arrival order, and the values are within the `multithread` tolerance (at most 1.8e-14 absolute).
- **Upstream tests (U):** upstream's own `tests/python_package_test`, run unmodified through the import shim.
- **Rust tests (R):** `cargo test -p lgbm-core`, including the ported C++ tests.

Upstream-suite totals: **1,272 passed, 0 failed, 49 unsupported, 19 skipped**. `test_sklearn.py` accounts for 731 passed (228 of them scikit-learn's estimator checks in `test_sklearn_integration`), 1 unsupported and 16 skipped. `test_dask.py` (32 functions) is skipped because dask is not installed.

## Performance

**Setup.**

- **Host:** a single machine (WSL 2 Ubuntu, 16 logical CPUs).
- **Data:** synthetic, 100 iterations, `force_row_wise=True`, and otherwise identical parameters.
- **Tasks:**
  - `binary` (1,000,000 rows, 28 dense features)
  - `regression` (1,000,000 rows, 28 dense features)
  - `binary_255` (1,000,000 rows, 28 dense features, `num_leaves=255`, `min_data_in_leaf=100`)
  - `binary_wide` (1,000,000 rows, 200 dense features)
  - `sparse` (1,000,000 rows, 100 features, scipy CSR with 5% nonzeros)
  - `wide_sparse` (250,000 rows, 5,000 features, scipy CSR with 0.2% nonzeros)
- **Timing:** the engines are interleaved, each run is a fresh process, and each time is the best of 3 runs.
- **Memory:** the process's peak resident set size minus its resident set size after generating the input (so it excludes the input matrix), the maximum over the 3 runs.
- **Predictions:** identical except for regression above 1 thread. There they differ by up to 1.8e-15 because of upstream's non-deterministic `BoostFromScore` reduction (see `docs/TESTING.md`).

Times are in seconds and memory in MB (upstream / lightgbm-rust); the ratio is rust / upstream training time.

- **Thread counts.** Upstream's 16-thread training time is erratic on this host, so its 16-thread ratios overstate the gap. The 8-thread ratios (0.75 to 1.14) are the conservative comparison.
- **Where lightgbm-rust is slower.** Training on `wide_sparse` at 4 and 8 threads (ratios 1.04 and 1.14), construction on `wide_sparse`, prediction on `sparse` above 1 thread, and prediction on `wide_sparse` at every thread count. Neither cause has been profiled yet.
- **Memory.** lightgbm-rust uses about 30 to 130 MB more working memory than upstream on most tasks, and the gap grows with the thread count. The `binary_wide` 8-thread value (74 MB) is below its own 1- and 4-thread values; it is likely an artifact of the host reclaiming pages during that run.
- **Scope.** These numbers are indicative only: they come from one host, with no isolation from other load. Bagging and feature subsampling are not benchmarked yet.

## Unresolved items and assumptions

- **Error-message prefixes.** Core error messages carry a prefix (`invalid data: ...`). Upstream tests match with `re.search`, so the prefix does not affect them, but the strings are not byte-identical to upstream's `LightGBMError` text.
- **Log lines not emitted.** Upstream's timing-based messages, its CLI output and the 1 GB read-progress line are not written (see the logging row). The other messages of the ported code paths are written at upstream's level and position, but only the paths reached by `test_logs`' 19 scenarios are compared with upstream.
- **Small differential datasets.** The differential cases use small synthetic data (≤ 8,000 rows). The 1e6-row benchmark checked prediction equality only, which is exact, not every intermediate value.
- **Untested platforms.** Native Windows and macOS builds have not been verified in this pass; all evidence above is from Linux (WSL 2).
